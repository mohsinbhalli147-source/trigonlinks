Also add a simple Notification feature to the Android Customer App.

Customers should receive notifications inside the app for important events, such as:

* New bill generated
* Bill due reminder
* Bill payment successful
* Complaint submitted
* Complaint status updated
* New announcement from the ISP
* Service suspended or restored

The Admin ERP should have a simple option to send announcements to customers.

A Notification screen should be available in the Android app where customers can see all previous notifications.

Keep the notification system simple, lightweight, and fully integrated with the existing ERP. Do not implement any unnecessary or overly complex notification system.
